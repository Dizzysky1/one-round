ROUND.

Every range issues you a single bullet. It punches through targets until its pierce runs out and rebounds off every wall and block until its ricochets run out. There is no second trigger pull — so the shot has to be planned, not aimed. Walk the floor, read the angles, then fire once. Time crawls while the round works.

Clear the range and you bank the leftovers as bonus, then pick one modification before the next one. Kills made after a ricochet score far more than kills made in a straight line.
